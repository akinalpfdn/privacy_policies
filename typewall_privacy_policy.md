# Privacy Policy - Typewall

**Last Updated:** September 29, 2026

## Summary

Typewall has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your notes are stored on your device. If you turn on sync, they are stored in your own iCloud or Google Drive. We run no servers and never receive your notes.

## What stays on your device

Your cards (titles and text), their positions, sizes and colors, the connections and groups between them, and your settings (theme, sync choice, keyboard shortcut and recently used custom colors) are stored locally in the app's own storage. None of this is sent to us.

## Optional sync

Sync is off until you choose a service in **Settings > Cloud Sync**.

- **iCloud** (iPhone, iPad and Mac): the app saves one file with your notes in Typewall's own container in your iCloud account. Apple stores and transfers it under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Google Drive**: you sign in with Google. The app asks only for the `drive.appdata` permission, which lets it read and write its own hidden folder in your Google Drive; it cannot see your other files. The app saves one file with your notes there. Google shows your account name and e-mail address during sign-in; the app does not store or send them anywhere. Sign-in tokens are kept on your device by Google Sign-In and are used only to talk to Google Drive directly from the app. Google stores the file under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Synced data travels directly between your device and Apple or Google, encrypted over TLS. We have no copy of it and no access to it.

Choosing **Off** in Settings > Cloud Sync stops syncing and signs you out of Google. You can also remove Typewall's access to your Google account at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Backups you export

**Save to Files** and **Share Backup** create a file with your notes. It goes only where you choose to save or send it.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect any personal data. There are no analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs, no advertising identifier, no in-app purchases, and no tracking across other apps or websites. The app does not ask for access to your camera, photos, contacts, microphone or location.

## Who processes the data

Only if you turn on sync: Apple (iCloud) or Google (Google Drive and Google Sign-In) store your synced notes in your own account under their privacy policies. Apple and Google also process the app's distribution as the operators of the App Store and Google Play. We do not sell data and we do not share it with anyone.

## Legal basis and retention

Where the GDPR or the Turkish KVKK applies: we do not process your personal data ourselves. Sync is carried out by Apple or Google in your account, at your request.

Notes stay on your device until you delete them or the app. Synced copies stay in your iCloud or Google Drive until you delete them.

## Deleting your data

- **On your device:** delete the cards in the app, or delete the app.
- **iCloud:** on iPhone or iPad, open Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage > Typewall and delete its data.
- **Google Drive:** in Google Drive on the web, open Settings > Manage apps > Typewall > Options > Delete hidden app data.

Because we hold no copy of your notes, we cannot delete or recover them for you, but we are happy to help with any of these steps.

## Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it collects, this document and its date will be updated before the new version is released.
